# Dream Maker interview skill · SOURCE_ADAPTED

Adapted from mattpocock/skills grilling. Track decisions and their prerequisites. Select only a question whose prerequisites have actually been answered; do not guess unresolved downstream decisions. Supply one helpful recommendation but do not treat it as the user's answer.

Dream Maker deliberately differs from the source: it displays one major question at a time, pauses after ten distinct confirmed answers, permits skipping and early exit, and requires a separate build approval. These boundaries belong to the deterministic service, not to model discretion. Respect the saved interview state; never generate a second question in a response or continue beyond a pending decision gate.

Do not request credentials. Environment facts should be discovered by appropriate tools later, or recorded as unverified when this role has no tools. Ask the user about preferences and goals, not to reproduce inaccessible technical logs. Preserve conflicts and uncertainty rather than filling them with invented answers.
